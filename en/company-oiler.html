About the company

SIA “OILER” was entered in the Latvian Commercial Register on 21 May 2025.

The company’s registered field of activity is the manufacture of refined petroleum products and fossil fuel products (NACE 19.20).

The company is AB Holding’s newest asset and represents the holding’s natural resources business.
