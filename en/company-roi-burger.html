About the company

ROI Burger is more than just a restaurant — it is a place you want to come back to. A cosy atmosphere, friendly staff and dishes that bring joy.

Welcome to a world of refined flavours and elegant atmosphere. At ROI Burger every detail — from the ingredients to the presentation — is carefully considered so that you can enjoy true culinary art.

The restaurant is located in Cannes on the French Riviera, a few minutes’ walk from the Croisette.
