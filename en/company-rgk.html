About the company

Rēzeknes gaļas kombināts was founded in 2001. At present, by volume of finished production, RGK holds a leading position in the Latvian market.

Today RGK is

  • A full-cycle meat processing company
  • A production facility compliant with EU requirements
  • Thermal chambers for cold-smoked sausages
  • Canning workshop
  • Frozen products workshop
  • Semi-finished products workshop

History

Our history

  1. 2001

    The company was established with approx. 25 employees and assortment of about 20 products.

  2. 2002

    HACCP system was implemented.

  3. 2004

    Rezekne meat factory Ltd. was awarded with prize "Gazele" as fastest growing company in Latvia.

  4. 2006

    After modernization process, the company has turned into all EU compliant manufacturing facility with a modern slaughterhouse.

  5. 2008

    Raznas cooked sausage was awarded as «Favorite product in RIMI store’s meat production group».

  6. 2009

    Canned production manufacture started to work. Result - increasing assortment range and export volumes.

  7. 2010

    The company has launched a production plant in Pinki for the manufacturing of frozen products, including dumplings.

  8. 2011

    Alkšņu scalded sausages got award «Product of the year».

  9. 2011

    Rezekne meat factory Ltd. was the first one in Latvia to start collaboration with Latvian Medical Association and produced series without the E products.

  10. 2013

    Products of RGK got included in the list of Forbes Top 5 most purchased products in Latvia.
