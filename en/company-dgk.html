About the company

SIA “Meat Union” was founded in 2010. Since 2011 the company has operated in the Russian market and has been actively developing its position. It has carried out a partial reconstruction of its production facility and built a new product warehouse, and an European Union project to increase production capacity is also under way.

The company currently produces around 15 tons of finished goods per day, with a potential of around 30 tons per day.

Meat Union is successfully developing its products under the “Dinaburga” brand, which has already established its position in the Russian market. The company’s range includes frankfurters, sausages, boiled sausages, dried and semi-dried sausages, cervelats, cold-smoked sausages, smoked pork and more.

Our mission.

Meat Union works to produce high-quality, flavourful products that are safe for the customer. Every employee makes compliance with all necessary standards a priority in order to ensure consistently high product quality, and continually develops their skills and professionalism. Meat Union constantly works on modernising production and improving processing technology.
