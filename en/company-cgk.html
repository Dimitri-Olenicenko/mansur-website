About the company

“Cēsu gaļas kombināts” is a modern, high-capacity meat processing company in Latvia, focused on the highest quality and compliant with European standards. The company was founded in February 2016 and its guiding aim is to improve, expand and develop.

“Cēsu gaļas kombināts” can take pride in its strong position in the Latvian market, built on honest and trustworthy relations between partners at every stage of cooperation — from purchasing cattle to fulfilling customers’ wishes.

It matters to us that only the best and freshest meat products reach the tables of meat lovers!
