Octo B

A road transport service at the Terehova border — parking, accommodation and facilities for hauliers.

About the company

SIA “Octo B” operates in Terehova, Ludza municipality — at Latvia’s largest and busiest freight border crossing.

The company provides parking for cars and lorries, catering, hotel rooms and showers.

Hygiene facilities also include laundry and drying — services that matter to hauliers during long waits in the queue.
