About the company

SIA “AZ-INTER” operates both as a main contractor and as a subcontractor, performing construction and design works, cost estimation, technical and economic analysis of construction solutions, approval of design documents and obtaining permits, construction, reconstruction and restoration of buildings, technical supervision and quality control of construction works, commissioning, project management, and servicing of facilities after handover.

Over the course of its work the construction firm has developed and improved, attracting qualified engineers and project managers. It has obtained certificates for managing construction works, managing and supervising restoration works, managing road construction, occupational health and safety, fire safety and protection, and cost estimation.
