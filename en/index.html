About the company

Nine companies, one strategy

The holding comprises several companies, including: Rēzeknes gaļas kombināts, Cēsu gaļas kombināts, Meat Union, the ROI restaurant and the construction company AZ Inter.

Products from our facilities are exported to 30 countries worldwide, while in Latvia our clients include Rimi, Maxima, Lido and many others. The holding’s turnover currently stands at 150 million euros, and our goal is to raise it to 1 billion in the near future.

Nine companies across five industries — from meat processing and restaurants to construction, logistics brokerage and natural resources.
